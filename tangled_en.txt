Story

An old witch named Gothel is the only one to see a drop of pure sunlight fall to the ground,
creating a magical flower with the ability to heal the sick and wounded, and she uses it
to keep herself young when she sings to the Flower. Hundreds of years later, the queen of
a nearby kingdom falls ill while expecting a child. Her guards, in search of a cure, find
the mysterious flower. They make a potion with the flower, which heals the queen, and she
gives birth to a girl named Rapunzel. Gothel discovers that Rapunzel’s golden hair retains
the healing power of the flower, as long as it is not cut, so she kidnaps the child and
isolates her in a tower, raising her as her own daughter. Every year on Rapunzel’s birthday,
the kingdom sends thousands of lanterns into the sky in the hope that one day the lost princess
will return.

Eighteen years later, Rapunzel lives in the tower with her pet chameleon, Pascal.
Rapunzel asks Gothel to let her go see the floating lights on her birthday, but Gothel denies
the request, saying that the world is full of people who covet her powers for their own gain.
Meanwhile, a group of thieves led by Flynn Rider steal the absent princess’s crown from the castle.
Flynn abandons his accomplices and stumbles upon Rapunzel’s tower, choosing it as a hiding place.
During the chase, the guard’s horse, Maximus, gets separated from the others and continues to hunt
Flynn on his own. Inside the tower, Flynn is knocked out by Rapunzel, who hides him.

Rapunzel tells Gothel that she wants a special paint for her birthday. Gothel agrees, and
after she leaves, Rapunzel makes a deal with Flynn—if he takes her to see the lights and brings
her back safely, she will return the crown to him. As Rapunzel becomes excited, she quickly
finds herself torn between her freedom and the guilt of disobedience. During their journey,
they visit the Snuggly Duckling tavern, which seems full of thugs just as Gothel had warned
Rapunzel, but all of them have their own dreams, just like Rapunzel. The royal guards continue
their search, and Gothel, growing suspicious of Rapunzel, returns to the tower and finds it empty.
The tavern’s patrons help Flynn and Rapunzel escape from the guards, but they end up cornered in a
dam, which Maximus accidentally destroys. Trapped in a flooding cave, Rapunzel reveals her powers
to Flynn, and her hair glows like the flower. This helps them escape safely, and then Flynn reveals
his real name, José Bezerra, along with the reason why he became a thief. While Flynn (now called
by Rapunzel by his real name) gathers firewood, Gothel secretly meets with Rapunzel. Gothel insists
he is only with her because of the crown, and tells her she should give it back to him as a test.

The next morning, Maximus confronts José again, but befriends Rapunzel and travels with the group
to the royal kingdom. That night, Rapunzel returns the crown to José, but he realizes, illuminated
by the lanterns, that Rapunzel is what he truly wants. Seeing his former accomplices, he tries to
return the crown to them, but the duo are now working with Gothel. They make it appear that José
is escaping with the crown, and Gothel, betraying the thieves, “rescues” Rapunzel and takes her back
to the tower. José, sentenced to be hanged for his crimes, discovers the betrayal from his captured
henchmen. The tavern folks rescue José, and he hurries to Rapunzel in the tower.

Meanwhile, Rapunzel discovers that she is the long-lost princess, and she confronts Gothel with
the revelation. José climbs the tower, but upon entering, he sees Rapunzel chained and gagged, and
he is stabbed by Gothel. Rapunzel promises to stay with Gothel forever if she lets her heal him.
Gothel agrees, but before Rapunzel can heal him, José says he would rather die than see her imprisoned
forever, and he cuts off Rapunzel’s long hair, destroying its healing power. Gothel’s true age begins
to return, and, screaming in horror, she trips on Rapunzel’s hair and falls from the tower window,
turning to dust before reaching the ground. José declares his love for Rapunzel and dies.
Heartbroken, she tries to sing the enchantment, but the power of her hair is gone. A tear falls onto
José’s face and miraculously heals him. They return to the castle, where Rapunzel reunites with the
royal family. A great celebration is held in honor of Rapunzel’s return. Rapunzel and José marry,
live happily ever after, and everyone fulfills their individual dreams.
